# Discovery prompts and candidate workflows

Use only the segment relevant to the request. Everything below is a hypothesis to investigate, not a feature commitment or a claim about Romanian businesses.

## Sweet shops and small specialty retailers

Investigate stock by piece/weight, expiry and batches, supplier receipts, recipes versus sellable stock, waste/spoilage, custom cake orders and deposits, pickup deadlines, seasonal demand, and reconciliation with existing sales/fiscal software. Distinguish a reseller from a producer: recipes, production planning, and food traceability may have different operational and regulatory implications. Verify obligations before recommending a compliant workflow.

## Hair salons and service businesses

Investigate booking conflicts, service duration, staff availability, customer reminders and permission, no-shows, deposits, recurring clients, service consumables, retail products, and owner visibility. Separate employee scheduling from commissions/payroll. Verify privacy and communications requirements and avoid collecting sensitive client information by default.

## Interview sequence

1. Tell me about the last time this problem happened. What started it?
2. Show or describe each step and the tools or people involved. Use redacted examples.
3. How often does this happen? What happens when it goes wrong?
4. What time, direct cost, lost margin, or missed capacity does it create? Which numbers can we verify?
5. What have you tried? What do you currently pay for, and why did you keep or abandon it?
6. Who decides on software? What would prevent switching? What must work on day one?
7. Would you participate in a concrete pilot? What outcome would justify continuing or paying?

Avoid leading questions such as “Would an AI dashboard be useful?” Do not count compliments or hypothetical willingness as purchase intent. Ask permission before recording or contacting anyone; keep personal details out of research artifacts unless necessary and authorized.

## Opportunity record

Use a compact table or narrative:

`Segment | Buyer/operator | Job/trigger | Problem | Current workaround | Evidence/date | Frequency | Cost/value assumption | Alternatives | Confidence | Experiment | Decision threshold`

Capture evidence IDs or source links so a product owner can trace each recommendation. When using interview data, report sample composition and limits. Do not generalize one salon or shop to the whole Romanian SMB market.
